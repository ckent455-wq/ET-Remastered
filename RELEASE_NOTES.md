# Far From Home v0.5.0 - Abandoned Blackwood

A playable Windows survival-horror preview with redesigned, water-damaged town interiors, a hilltop chapel and dark cellar quest, flashlight batteries, and the animated crow at the Blackwood sign.

## Play

Download **FarFromHome-v0.5.0-Windows-x64.zip**, extract the whole folder, and run **FarFromHome.exe**. Keep the EXE, PCK and bundled DLLs together. Godot is not required. Controls, asset credits, licenses, build information and SHA256SUMS.txt are included.

## New since v0.4.0

- All six town buildings have coordinated furniture layouts, wall finishes, ceilings and lighting. The apartment has sleeping, living, dining and kitchen areas; the clinic has tiled floors and a privacy curtain; the shop has stocked shelves and a continuous counter.
- Ceiling leaks, streaked damp plaster, mold, fallen plaster, broken furniture, shattered dishes, half-packed bags and spilled supplies show a hurried departure.
- The chapel sits uphill off Main Street, reached by a rebuilt winding road, entrance court and footpath. Its doors open through player interaction.
- The powered workshop cabinet also supplies the chapel cellar key. Recover the tuning unit in the dark cellar, then repair the radio with both quest parts.
- A quieter HUD keeps inventory details in the journal. The flashlight has a charge bar and needs collected batteries; Q fits one. The flashlight lowers and returns when switched off and on, with no arm model.
- Police emergency lights cast dim red and blue glows.
- A crow perches on the town sign, calls, flies away when approached and returns later. Shooting it triggers the persistent achievement "Wow, what an asshole". The notification also appears when an already-earned achievement is triggered.

## Preview scope

Windows 10/11 x64; ANGLE/Direct3D11 compatibility rendering; 60 FPS cap; PS2-style 640 x 400 presentation. Journey progress resets on a new game; achievements persist. Drones remain disabled. Start unarmed at the crash, find supplies in Blackwood, recover both radio parts and hold E near the repaired radio to call for rescue.

Validation includes quest completion and restart, storage interactions, interior access, NPC patrols, camera stability, chapel access and cellar lighting, crow behavior and achievements, plus the exported build and final ZIP startup.
