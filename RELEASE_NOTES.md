# Far From Home v0.7.0 - Movement and Hurt Reactions

A playable Windows survival-horror preview with new movement controls and player damage voices.

## Play

Download **FarFromHome-v0.7.0-Windows-x64.zip**, extract the whole folder and run **FarFromHome.exe**. Keep the EXE, PCK and bundled DLLs together. Godot is not required. Instructions, credits, licenses and build information are included.

## New since v0.6.0

- Press Space to jump; hold Ctrl to crouch. Crouching lowers the camera smoothly, slows movement and quiets footsteps. Low ceilings prevent standing until there is room. Crouching works in the chapel cellar, too.
- Three supplied voice recordings now play when the player takes damage. All three rotate without immediate repeats, with balanced volume. Missed swings, invulnerable contact and healing do not trigger them. Pausing suspends the voice; fatal hits can finish their reaction.
- Updated the controls screen and player instructions for jump and crouch.

Includes the Blackwood signal storyline, three respawning woodland enemies, scavenged ammunition, keyed shotgun crate, chapel basement rifle, abandoned interiors, crow behavior and both radio endings from v0.6.0.

## Preview scope

Windows 10/11 x64; ANGLE/Direct3D11 compatibility rendering; 60 FPS cap; PS2-style 640 x 400 presentation. Journey progress resets on a new game; achievements persist. Start unarmed at the crash, scavenge in Blackwood, recover the radio parts and call for rescue.

Validation includes movement and ceiling clearance, hurt voices and actual melee damage, existing gameplay regressions, and standalone Windows startup from the packaged ZIP.
