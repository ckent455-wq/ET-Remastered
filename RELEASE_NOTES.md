# Far From Home v1.5.0 - Silence the Signal

Playable Windows x64 survival-horror preview, built with Godot 4.7.2.

## Changes since v1.4.0

- Finish by shutting down the independently powered chapel repeater at its basement MAIN ISOLATOR, then contact county through the station's GREEN bypass. Shutdown silences the carrier and prevents defeated chapel creatures from reviving. Objectives, navigation, notes and dialogue explain the required steps.
- Pat, Aubrey and John each have two lore questions and progress-aware advice in their own voice. Aubrey speaks about her own medical records; John explains the equipment; Pat focuses on routes and safe rescue contact.
- Optional side quest: collect Ada's letter from Saint George Hotel and deliver it to John for 12 pistol rounds. Full packs or reserves leave unclaimed ammunition with John for later collection. The journal tracks the quest and retains the letter transcript.
- Forty downloaded PSX footstep clips now follow concrete, wood, grass, dirt, gravel, stone, metal and stair surfaces, with varied samples and movement-dependent volume.
- Expanded continuity: John inspected the chapel basement before the creatures arrived, Pat explains the temporary mausoleum supply cache, and the road log accounts for his three days of missing time under the signal.
- The house and tunnel creatures are reanimated dead, distinct from the transformed chapel residents. A broken Victorian basement foundation reveals a short dug burrow, rubble, burial cloth and a solid collapsed end that blocks player access.
- Restored the police car's opaque hood and trunk while retaining its opening driver door and seated pistol. Corrected the fuse-placement encounter message without changing its trigger.

## Play

Download **FarFromHome-v1.5.0-Windows-x64.zip**, extract it completely, and run **FarFromHome.exe**. Keep the EXE and PCK together. Godot is not required. The included README explains controls and testing mode.

This is a development preview. Asset credits, engine licenses, build information and SHA256 checksums accompany the download. The in-game update checker supports this release.
