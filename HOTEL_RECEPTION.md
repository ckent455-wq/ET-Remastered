# Saint George Hotel reception

A 1920s-inspired walnut reception counter now occupies the cleared right-hand lobby area. The counter, key/mail rack and clerk chair are editable roots under Furniture in editing/hotel_layout.tscn. Move their complete roots to keep details and collision together. The guest register, brass service bell, banker lamp and candlestick telephone are decorative. Ada's existing letter remains a collectible, now resting on the counter. The left end of the desk remains open for staff access.

Historical visual references only; no external meshes or photographs were copied:
- Roosevelt Hotel front desk, published 1925: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roosevelt_Hotel_front_desk.png
- Read House original mail cubbyholes, 1926: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Read_House_Hotel_Front_Desk_Cubbyholes.jpg

Original low-poly counter and accessories built locally using Godot geometry and generated walnut grain. Hanging decorative keys reuse the project's existing key model; the clerk chair reuses the supplied apartment asset.
