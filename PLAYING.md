# Far From Home — Blackwood Chapel Preview v0.4.0

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Godot is not required to play.

Extract the complete ZIP to a folder, then double-click **FarFromHome.exe**.
Keep FarFromHome.exe and FarFromHome.pck together. The game starts at the studio
presentation and title screen; choose **Enter Blackwood** or press Enter.

You survived a car accident on Old Mill Road. Follow the road into Blackwood,
find supplies and weapons, and repair the town radio to call for rescue.
You begin with a flashlight and no gun.

The stone chapel uphill along Chapel Road from the north end of Main Street is open for exploration and
marked on the journal map. The dense forest forms a closed boundary around town.

## Controls

- Mouse: look; WASD / arrows: move; Shift: sprint.
- E: inspect, search, collect, open doors, and talk.
- F: flashlight; Tab: journal and map; Escape: pause.
- Left mouse: fire; right mouse: aim; R: reload.
- 1 / 2 / 3: switch between guns you have found; 0: holster.
- H: first-aid kit; Q: flashlight battery; M: mute / unmute.
- After repairing the receiver, hold E near it inside the radio station to call for help.

The pistol is in the ranger office, the rifle in the workshop, and the shotgun in
the millkeeper's rooms in the old hotel. Read the journal and speak to the townspeople for radio-repair clues.

This is an early playable preview. It includes the crash opening, town exploration,
searchable interiors, weapon pickups, NPC patrols and death animations, and the
radio-rescue quest. There are currently no drones. Progress resets for each new journey.

The image intentionally uses PS2-style low-poly assets, low-resolution textures,
fog, and a 640 × 400 viewport. The Windows build uses ANGLE/Direct3D11 and a 60 FPS cap.

The flashlight bar shows remaining charge. Switch it off with F to conserve power.
Find batteries in town storage and press Q to fit one; an empty flashlight stays
off until refilled. TAB shows health kits, spare batteries and your objective.
Chapel Road ends at a grassy court; follow the footpath and press E at the doors.

Search the powered workshop coil cabinet for the chapel cellar key. Follow Chapel
Road uphill and find the locked cellar door at the back right inside the church.
E unlocks it; E again descends. Recover the tuning unit from the cellar workbench,
then use the stairs to return. The radio needs both the tuning unit and signal coil.
