# Far From Home - Survival Horror Preview

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Godot is not required to play.

Extract the complete ZIP to a folder, then double-click **FarFromHome.exe**.
Keep FarFromHome.exe and FarFromHome.pck together. The game starts at the studio
presentation and title screen; choose **Enter Blackwood** or press Enter.
The game opens in a resizable window with minimize and maximize controls. **F11** or **Alt+Enter** switches between full screen and a window. The internal retro resolution is scaled to fit the display.

You survived a car accident on Old Mill Road. Follow the road into Blackwood,
find supplies and weapons, and repair the town radio to call for rescue.
You wake in the driver's seat. Press E to try the ignition twice, then advance the dialogue and press E to open the door and get out. Head down Old Mill Road toward town for help. There is no automatic gear reminder on your first exit.

The crash leaves you at **25% health**. Just beyond the cemetery gate, your vision dims, movement staggers and footsteps slow. You drop to your knees and black out before the tree scare. The player's light-headed dialogue clears after two seconds. A 5.5-second blackout ends with a three-dot countdown, then your eyes blink open in the pharmacy's medical bed, where Aubrey treats your head wound and explains Blackwood's broadcast. This rescue is the required normal opening; testing mode skips it.

After leaving the bed, retrieve any missing backpack and flashlight from the car trunk. The prompt names what you still need. Your belongings remain where you left them. The first spare bandage becomes available after Aubrey's treatment: in your backpack if you already collected it, or when you retrieve it afterward. H uses bandages first (+10 health), then medpacks (+50 health); full health preserves supplies. More bandages are in the clinic's bedside drawer. The rescue happens once per journey and persists through saves. Aubrey remembers the bedside introduction.

The chapel uphill along Chapel Road from the north end of Main Street is open for exploration and
marked on the journal map. The dense forest forms a closed boundary around town.

Talk to **Pat** about Blackwood and the old house, **Aubrey** about the signal and her missing patients, and **John** about the church equipment and Ada. Pat and Aubrey offer two lore questions and current advice. John also explains where the broadcast began. After each NPC's introduction, use Up/Down and E/Enter, click a question, or use the controller's D-pad/left stick and confirm button. Answers return to the question list; Esc/back returns to the list or leaves. Answers are summarized under the speaker's name in the journal. Pat still offers the mausoleum key when needed.

## Testing mode

Press **F10** or use **Testing Mode** in the pause menu to toggle testing on or off. Testing starts immediately without the intro or wake-up sequence. It grants every weapon, key, quest item and tool, unlocks restricted areas, and provides unlimited health, ammo, stamina and flashlight charge. The backpack shows a testing equipment list with capacity limits disabled.

Turning testing off restores the equipment, supplies, health and lock access you had before enabling it. Exploration, combat and story events are not rewound. Restart Game begins a normal journey with testing off. Testing is off by default on every launch.

## Controls

- Mouse: look; WASD / arrows: move; Shift: sprint.
- Space: jump. Hold Ctrl: crouch; release to stand when there is room.
- E: inspect, search, collect, open/close doors from either side, and talk. Building doors swing inward.
- I: backpack (drag items to arrange them; R rotates the selected or held item). F: flashlight; Tab: journal and map; Escape: pause, with Restart Game and Main Menu options.
- C inside the journal: case notes and evidence leads.
- Left mouse: fire, or punch while unarmed; right mouse: aim; R: reload.
- 1 / 2 / 3: switch between guns you have found; 0: holster.
- H: bandage / first-aid kit; Q: flashlight battery; M: mute / unmute.
- After repairing the receiver, hold E near it inside the radio station to call for help.

a keyed box at the back of the ranger office. The box key is on the Saint George Hotel bedroom dresser. Read the journal and speak to the townspeople for radio-repair clues.

## Controller

Use any standard Godot-compatible controller. Button prompts automatically switch when you use the controller or keyboard/mouse; Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo labels are supported.

- Left stick: move; click left stick: sprint. Right stick: look.
- A / Cross: jump; B / Circle (hold): crouch; X / Square: interact, talk, try the ignition, get out of the car.
- LT / L2: aim; RT / R2: fire or punch while unarmed; Y / Triangle: reload.
- LB / RB (L1 / R1): cycle weapons; click right stick: holster.
- D-pad down: flashlight; up: journal/map; left: first aid; right: spare battery.
- View / Touchpad: backpack; Start / Options: pause.
- Menus: left stick or D-pad to select, A / Cross to confirm, B / Circle to go back.
- Backpack: D-pad or left stick selects; X / Square picks up an item, moves it, and places it. B / Circle cancels a move or closes the bag. A / Cross uses, Y / Triangle examines, LB / L1 combines, RB / R1 discards (confirm twice). Close the bag and press D-pad up for the journal/map.
- Journal: LB / RB turns pages; Y / Triangle toggles case notes; B / Circle closes.

On Nintendo controllers, the corresponding physical bottom/right/left/top face buttons are B/A/Y/X. Disconnecting the active controller pauses gameplay.

An optional rusted shovel leans against the workshop wall and occupies a 1 x 3 backpack space. Find the one freshly disturbed grave in Old Mill Cemetery and hold E (Xbox X / PlayStation Square) while looking at it to dig. Releasing interact cancels the digging. The uncovered cache contains six pistol rounds and a folded note; the other graves cannot be dug.

With no gun equipped, use the fire control to throw alternating punches. Punches deal 12 damage within 1.8 meters, with a short windup and recovery. Fists start lowered. Press 0 (R3 on controller) to raise or lower them while unarmed. If holding a gun, press once to holster it, then again to raise fists. Fire does nothing while fists are lowered.

The Victorian house now has two separate Wrapper encounters: one upstairs and one in its basement. They use normal weapon and melee damage, stay on their own floors, and remain dead until a new journey.

An optional Straw Reaper encounter waits at the deep forest boundary beyond the cemetery east/rear fence. Approaching that edge triggers it from cover. It stays in the rear woods and remains dead until a new journey.

The mausoleum route contains three Wrappers: one near the entrance, one halfway through the passage, and one concealed behind a storage screen in the open cellar at the far end. They wake separately and cannot follow you through the exit portals.

## Updates

Choose **Check for Updates** on the main menu. The game checks the ET-Remastered GitHub repository for a newer published Windows release, including previews. Choose **Download Update**, then **Install & Restart**. Downloads are checked against GitHub's SHA-256 digest, and the installer verifies the packaged EXE and PCK before replacing the game.

The updater backs up the current build in a `.ffh-update-backup-*` folder beside the EXE. If copying fails, it restores the old files. Achievements remain in the game's user-data folder. The complete portable game folder must be writable; the updater never requests administrator access. Update logs are in `%APPDATA%/Godot/app_userdata/Far From Home/updates/install.log`.

Running the project in Godot supports checking releases; installation is available in exported Windows builds. The updater does not overwrite the source checkout. Updates are installed only when selected, and a failed or cancelled download leaves the installed game untouched.

Optional side quest: ask John about Ada, collect her letter from Saint George Hotel (one backpack cell), and interact with John to give it to him. The journal records the task and completion; you keep a readable transcript after handing it over.

John rewards delivery of Ada's letter with **12 pistol rounds**. If your backpack or reserve is full, he holds the remainder; talk to him again after making room.

Footsteps now follow the surface beneath you: concrete paving, wooden floors, forest grass, loose gravel, dirt, stone, metal and stairs. Sprinting is louder; crouching is quieter.

To finish: collect the radio parts, **shut down the chapel basement repeater at MAIN ISOLATOR**, repair the station receiver, select **GREEN / DIRECT COUNTY**, and hold E at the receiver. The chapel has independent power; repairing or switching off the station alone does not silence it. Once the chapel rack is off, its amber relay route cannot complete a call.

## Saving and loading

Press Esc and choose **SAVE / LOAD**. Select one of three slots, then SAVE or LOAD; replacing a save or the current paused journey requires a second press. The title menu has **LOAD JOURNEY**, and the death screen offers LOAD JOURNEY (L on keyboard, Y/Triangle on controller). Keyboard arrows/Tab and Enter, mouse, or controller D-pad/stick and confirm navigate the screen.

Saves retain your position (including basements), health, flashlight, inventory, weapon ammo, quests, journal, searched supplies, doors, NPCs, enemies and scripted-event progress. Each overwritten slot keeps a verified backup that is offered if its primary save is damaged. Achievements remain stored separately. Saves are local to this Windows user and remain outside the game install folder. Finish the seated opening and take your backpack before saving; testing-mode sessions and incomplete scripted sequences cannot be saved.

Backpack rotation: press R on keyboard. On a controller, pick up an item with Square/X, press Triangle/Y to rotate, then Square/X to place or Circle/B to cancel. Rotations obey the grid boundaries and occupied spaces and are saved with your layout.

Pursuing town creatures can pound on an unlocked building door for several seconds before forcing it open. Mummies push unlocked hinged doors open more slowly. Locked or bolted passage doors remain secure. You can still close a forced door manually.

## Combat and pursuit

Monsters keep pursuing once alerted and can force unlocked doors, follow stairs and use opened underground passages. Locked routes remain barriers. Standard creatures take about six pistol body hits, three rifle hits or two close shotgun blasts. Headshots do 1.5x damage. Mummies resist staggering more strongly, and shotgun damage falls off beyond five meters. Use normal mode to judge difficulty; testing mode grants unlimited health and ammunition.

Doors, conversations, searchable storage and item interactions work before you retrieve your backpack. The backpack screen and flashlight require their corresponding gear; key-locked routes still require their keys.
