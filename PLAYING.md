# Far From Home - Survival Horror Preview

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Godot is not required to play.

Extract the complete ZIP to a folder, then double-click **FarFromHome.exe**.
Keep FarFromHome.exe and FarFromHome.pck together. The game starts at the studio
presentation and title screen; choose **Enter Blackwood** or press Enter.

You survived a car accident on Old Mill Road. Follow the road into Blackwood,
find supplies and weapons, and repair the town radio to call for rescue.
After the wake-up dialogue, press E to stand. Open the crashed car with E, then collect your backpack, flashlight and detour sheet. You begin without weapons. You can close and reopen the car door.

The chapel uphill along Chapel Road from the north end of Main Street is open for exploration and
marked on the journal map. The dense forest forms a closed boundary around town.

## Controls

- Mouse: look; WASD / arrows: move; Shift: sprint.
- Space: jump. Hold Ctrl: crouch; release to stand when there is room.
- E: inspect, search, collect, open doors, and talk.
- I: backpack (drag items to arrange them). F: flashlight; Tab: journal and map; Escape: pause, with a Restart Game option.
- C inside the journal: case notes and evidence leads.
- Left mouse: fire, or punch while unarmed; right mouse: aim; R: reload.
- 1 / 2 / 3: switch between guns you have found; 0: holster.
- H: first-aid kit; Q: flashlight battery; M: mute / unmute.
- After repairing the receiver, hold E near it inside the radio station to call for help.

The pistol is in the ranger office, the service rifle in the locked chapel basement, and the shotgun in
a keyed box at the back of the ranger office. The box key is on the Saint George Hotel bedroom dresser. Read the journal and speak to the townspeople for radio-repair clues.

This is an early playable preview. It includes the crash opening, town exploration,
searchable interiors, weapon pickups, NPC patrols and death animations, and the
radio-rescue quest. There are currently no drones. Progress resets for each new journey.

The image intentionally uses PS2-style low-poly assets, low-resolution textures,
fog, and a 640 Ã— 400 viewport. The Windows build uses ANGLE/Direct3D11 and a 60 FPS cap.

The flashlight bar shows remaining charge. Switch it off with F to conserve power.
Find batteries in town storage and press Q to fit one; an empty flashlight stays
off until refilled. TAB shows health kits, spare batteries and your objective.
Chapel Road ends at a grassy court; follow the footpath and press E at the doors.

Restore workshop power and collect the signal coil. Speak to Pat for the mausoleum key, then search the Whitcomb mausoleum for the antenna coupler and chapel cellar key. Follow Chapel
Road uphill and find the locked cellar door at the back right inside the church.
Press E to unlock the door and descend. Recover the tuning unit from the cellar workbench,
then use the stairs to return. The radio needs the tuning unit, signal coil, and antenna coupler.

Inspect your wreck for the detour notice. Read documents and speak to Pat,
Aubrey and John to investigate the evacuation. The operator's instructions at the
radio station point to three records that explain a safer transmission procedure.
Set the small BYPASS switch on the station desk to GREEN for direct county
dispatch; AMBER still routes through the chapel repeater. Reading does not flip
the switch for you. Evidence is optional for completing a journey. The case-notes page tracks what you have learned without revealing unread
documents. A new journey clears story evidence along with the quest.

Mouse wheel or V cycles through collected guns; wheel up cycles backward.
Number keys 1, 2 and 3 still select guns directly; 0 holsters. Cycling skips
undiscovered guns and cancels a reload without adding or removing ammunition.

Creatures hide in the woods around the chapel. Find a weapon before heading
uphill. Listen for breathing from the undergrowth; they can stalk toward the
church court after you approach. Their growls warn of an encounter; a claw strike has a wind-up
and can be avoided by stepping back. Solid walls block their sight and attacks.
Gunshots attract them, and using your flashlight makes you easier to spot. Sprint
away or fight; H uses a collected first-aid kit. Menus and the journal pause enemies.

Visible active enemies have health bars. The service rifle hits twice as hard as
the pistol. Find it on the county lockup rack in the chapel basement; the cellar
key is still in the powered workshop coil cabinet.

Defeated woodland monsters return to their hiding places after two minutes of active play. Menus pause this countdown. There are three monsters around the chapel woods.

The shotgun is in a locked, hinged wooden box at the back of the ranger office. Find its rusty key on the Saint George Hotel bedroom dresser; unlock and open the box, then use E to take the gun. The service rifle remains in the chapel cellar.

The clinic sheet, Ada's letter, vestry book and cellar log explain how the chapel carrier gradually changed residents into the three woodland creatures. Read them in the journal; use C for case notes. The green desk bypass avoids the compromised relay when calling county.

Opening the keyed chapel cellar door takes you straight into the basement in one interaction. Returning upstairs shuts it without relocking; E opens it and takes you downstairs again.

AMMUNITION: The ranger pistol has only 3 loaded rounds and no reserve. Collect ammo cartons with E; R reloads from your reserve. Pistol cartons give 6 rounds, rifle cartons 12 rounds, and shotgun cartons 4 shells. Cartons can be collected before finding their weapon. New weapons give their loaded ammunition only, with no free reserve; previously collected rounds are retained. The journal shows all three reserve counts.

Ammo cartons are scattered through the store, hotel rooms, clinic, ranger office, workshop, radio station and chapel cellar. They stay collected for the current journey, and restore on a new journey.

OLD MILL CEMETERY: Follow the east/right footpath from Old Mill Road between the wreck and town. After restoring workshop power and collecting the signal coil, get the mausoleum key from Pat, unlock the Whitcomb mausoleum, and take the coupler inside. It occupies two backpack slots and is required alongside the coil and tuning unit to repair the station radio. Press I for the backpack; TAB opens the journal and updated map.


WORKSHOP POWER: Find the workshop fuse in the store counter drawer (one backpack slot). Follow the alley between the repair shop and Saint George Hotel into the enclosed rear yard, then use E to open the cabinet, E again to install the fuse, and E once more to flip the switch. Installing the fuse starts the monster encounter; the switch restores workshop power. Flashlight cells remain separate.

CRASH OPENING: A new journey begins with the driver waking after the crash. Advance the opening dialogue with E, Enter, or Space. Walk to the driver's door, aim at it, and press E to open it. Take the backpack, then the flashlight with E. I opens the collected backpack; F switches on the collected flashlight. Restart Game repeats this opening and puts both items back in the car.

DETOUR SHEET: After collecting the backpack and flashlight, pick up the printed sheet from the front seat with E. It disappears from the car and is saved in the journal. Inspecting the wreck does not collect it automatically. Restart puts it back on the seat.

The hotel basement door is bolted from the other side. Reach its cellar through the hidden tunnel inside the Whitcomb mausoleum, then unbolt the hotel door from below. Normal mausoleum key requirements apply. Restoring workshop power prepares an ambush when you return through the alley to Main Street; the townspeople run indoors first.

## Controller

Use any standard Godot-compatible controller. Button prompts automatically switch when you use the controller or keyboard/mouse; Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo labels are supported.

- Left stick: move; click left stick: sprint. Right stick: look.
- A / Cross: jump; B / Circle (hold): crouch; X / Square: interact, talk, stand up.
- LT / L2: aim; RT / R2: fire or punch while unarmed; Y / Triangle: reload.
- LB / RB (L1 / R1): cycle weapons; click right stick: holster.
- D-pad down: flashlight; up: journal/map; left: first aid; right: spare battery.
- View / Touchpad: backpack; Start / Options: pause.
- Menus: left stick or D-pad to select, A / Cross to confirm, B / Circle to go back.
- Backpack: D-pad or left stick selects; X / Square picks up an item, moves it, and places it. B / Circle cancels a move or closes the bag. A / Cross uses, Y / Triangle examines, LB / L1 combines, RB / R1 discards (confirm twice). Close the bag and press D-pad up for the journal/map.
- Journal: LB / RB turns pages; Y / Triangle toggles case notes; B / Circle closes.

On Nintendo controllers, the corresponding physical bottom/right/left/top face buttons are B/A/Y/X. Disconnecting the active controller pauses gameplay.

An optional rusted shovel leans against the workshop wall and occupies a 1 x 3 backpack space. Find the one freshly disturbed grave in Old Mill Cemetery and hold E (Xbox X / PlayStation Square) while looking at it to dig. Releasing interact cancels the digging. The uncovered cache contains six pistol rounds and a folded note; the other graves cannot be dug.

With no gun equipped, use the fire control to throw alternating punches. Punches deal 12 damage within 1.8 meters, with a short windup and recovery. Fists start lowered. Press 0 (R3 on controller) to raise or lower them while unarmed. If holding a gun, press once to holster it, then again to raise fists. Fire does nothing while fists are lowered.

The Victorian house now has two separate Wrapper encounters: one upstairs and one in its basement. They use normal weapon and melee damage, stay on their own floors, and remain dead until a new journey.

An optional Straw Reaper encounter waits at the deep forest boundary beyond the cemetery east/rear fence. Approaching that edge triggers it from cover. It stays in the rear woods and remains dead until a new journey.
