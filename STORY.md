# Blackwood story continuity

The chapel repeater spreads an unidentified carrier beneath familiar voices and recorded messages. Prolonged exposure first damages sleep and memory, then changes the body. The monsters are transformed residents, not an unrelated invading species. What produced the carrier after the storm remains unknown.

## Timeline

- Before the incident, neglected roofs already damaged the town interiors.
- One month before evacuation, a storm damages the civil-defense repeater beneath the chapel. A low tone begins in the relayed signal. Its buried line feeds the town station and porch speaker. The inspection is never completed.
- Over the following weeks, residents near running sets and loudspeakers lose sleep, hear borrowed voices, forget things and become confused or violent. Severe cases develop locked jaws and distorted hands. Exposure varies; early symptoms ease away from the signal. Ordinary radio is not inherently infectious.
- One week before evacuation, caretaker Jonah, who sleeps near the continuously running rack, changes and retreats into the woods. Father Bellamy witnesses it. His maintenance book admits that keeping the warning speaker on spread the carrier.
- Three nights before play, the clinic orders evacuation. Ruth, Ada and Father Bellamy leave on the final 01:40 southbound bus. Owen stays to secure the equipment; Mara and Elias search for the missing.
- At 02:10, Edith and Silas, two advanced patients who refuse the bus, walk uphill. Mara recognizes Edith's clinic bracelet on a changing wrist. Owen later records Jonah's work coat and Silas's splinted hand. These are the three creatures around the chapel.
- At 02:17, the loudspeaker plays a recorded bus horn. Elias reopens the barrier; no vehicle returns.
- At 02:20, Elias broadcasts "Three still here. Road open." This refers to Elias, Mara and Owen, the three remaining untransformed residents. The repeater later loops it. The Population 3 sign does not count the three transformed missing residents or the player.
- After the evacuation, Owen removes the town coil. That stops relayed town broadcasts, but the independently powered chapel rack remains active. The hill is the remaining concentration of the carrier. The creatures prevent him recovering the isolated county tuning unit.
- Owen observes that a shot stops a creature temporarily; while the carrier runs, it moves again about two minutes later. This explains the gameplay respawn without implying gunfire cures the transformation.
- Tonight, the player follows an undated detour sheet collected at a fuel stop, swerves around a figure and crashes outside town. Brief exposure is not instant transformation. No hidden infection timer is imposed on the player.

## Evidence and motivations

Nine existing records distribute the explanation: the clinic charts progressive symptoms; Ada documents Owen's early memory trouble and improvement away from the set; the store records wider confusion; the vestry book names the first transformation; the cellar test identifies all three creatures and explains their return. Mara's conversation responds to the clinic evidence. The investigation remains optional and all notes remain available in the journal.

Elias regrets reopening the barrier. Mara stays to search for her patients. Owen stays to isolate the equipment, then cannot get past the creatures to retrieve the tuner. Ada and Father Bellamy leave on the final bus. Evacuation was a response to weeks of symptoms, not the cause of the pre-existing decay.

## Radio logic and endings

The station needs the workshop coil and isolated cellar tuning unit. Repair alone restores power; it does not reroute the antenna. The physical desk BYPASS switch selects AMBER (chapel repeater, carrying the tone) or GREEN (direct county, avoiding it). Reading never toggles hardware. Hold E at the main receiver to call.

- AMBER: SOMETHING ANSWERED. Elias's old recording loops; no dispatcher confirms the player's location.
- GREEN: NO SIRENS. County acknowledges a quiet approach from Old Mill Road. The player reports surviving residents and switches the set off. This establishes rescue contact, not a cure or a complete explanation of the carrier's origin.

Sirens attract the transformed residents through ordinary sound; they are not the source of the original illness. The green channel avoids sending the compromised repeater's carrier to responders. Documents, survivor count and evidence percentage do not secretly gate either ending.
