# Sebus Hardware neon sign

Original 3D lettering, tubing, blue cabinet, pale border, weathered SEBUS BROTHERS panel and wall brackets referenced from the real projecting Hotpoint/APPLIANCES sign in Weston, Missouri.

Reference photograph: https://roadarch.com/10/9/hotpoint.jpg
Reference context: https://roadarch.com/signs/mo8.html#Hotpoint
The photograph is reference only and is not included in game assets.

Two readable faces use individual tube haze and a restrained nearby light. Random brief sputter bursts are separated by 6–17 seconds of steady illumination after an initial 5–12 second delay. The cabinet remains visible when the neon sputters. No collision is added over the pavement.

Local preview: `FarFromHome.exe -- --preview-sebus-sign`.
