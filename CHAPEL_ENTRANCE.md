# Chapel entrance

The entrance uses a straight, hand-laid flagstone walk with staggered joints, clipped stone corners, gravel margins that blend into grass, and a wider landing. The terrain remains the only outdoor walking collider; paving is visual and flush. Porch posts sit outside the clear doorway route.

The shallow timber portico and foundation planting take proportions from rural Missouri church entrances. Clapboard walls and pale fascia use restrained painted-wood materials. Posts and supporting beams use exposed weathered brown timber, with vertical grain on the posts; neither reuses the wall siding atlas. Existing local rock and forest assets supply paving grain and vegetation. No reference photographs are shipped as textures.

References:
- Yeakley Chapel, Missouri: https://www.ksmu.org/news/2022-08-15/along-old-route-66-a-congregation-celebrates-the-methodist-roots-of-the-ozarks
- Redwood Holiness Church, Missouri: https://www.doyledavidson.com/news/mo_pics/redwood2.shtml

Local preview: `FarFromHome.exe -- --preview-chapel-entry`. Normal launches retain the standard opening. `tools/check_chapel_approach.gd` checks three actual player walking lines from the road through the open doorway.


The garden borders reuse assets/forest/rock_0.glb, rock_1.glb and rock_2.glb. Each stone is fitted to a small irregular fieldstone shape and buried 4.5 cm into the soil. These decorative stones add no collision to the clear entrance route.

Bench: Quaternius' CC0 Bench (https://poly.pizza/m/jLxjFxFRpw), downloaded as assets/chapel_bench/park_bench.glb. Width fitted to 1.95 m, grounded and turned toward the road. The old block bench is hidden and its collision disabled. Model source and license are recorded beside the asset in CREDITS.txt.
